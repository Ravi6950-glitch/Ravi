# Board brief: GrowX Media

This brief stands alone. You cannot see the conversation it came from. Everything
known about the business is below; anything not listed here is unknown.

## The business

- Name: GrowX Media.
- What it is: a full-service marketing agency. Strategy, content, paid ads and
  account management are bundled together and sold as a single monthly retainer
  rather than as separate line items.
- Target customer: creators and personal brands — founders, coaches and
  influencers who are building an audience and selling their own products
  (courses, coaching, digital products, sponsorships).
- Price: one bundled retainer at ₹25,000–₹75,000 per month (roughly $300–900
  per month). The currency indicates an India-based or India-priced operation.
- Delivery: fully remote. Clients can be anywhere.
- Acquisition: inbound. The plan is for clients to find GrowX Media through its
  own social content and SEO — the agency's own audience is intended to serve as
  the proof that it can do the work. No outbound sales motion is planned.

## Stage

Pre-launch and about to open. No clients, no revenue, nothing operating yet.

## What is NOT known

Do not invent any of the following. If your lens needs one, name it as an open
question instead:

- Startup capital, runway, or any budget figure.
- Team size, who does the work, or whether anyone besides the founder exists.
- The founder's own current audience size or track record — note that the inbound
  plan depends entirely on this, and it has not been stated.
- Market size, number of competitors, or what competitors charge.
- Cost to deliver one retainer, or cost to acquire a client.
- Any client results or case studies.
