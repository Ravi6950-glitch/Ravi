# GrowX Media

- What it is: a full-service marketing agency that bundles strategy, content, paid ads and account management into one monthly retainer.
- Who it is for: creators and personal brands — founders, coaches and influencers who are building an audience and selling their own products.
- What it sells, at what price: one bundled monthly retainer, ₹25,000–₹75,000/month (roughly $300–900/month).
- Where and how: remote / online, clients anywhere. Delivered remotely. First clients are expected to arrive inbound — via GrowX Media's own social content and SEO, on the logic that the agency's own audience proves it can do the work.
- Budget and constraints: NOT STATED by the founder. Stage is pre-launch and about to open, with no clients and no revenue yet. Startup capital, runway, team size and the founder's own audience size are all unknown and must not be assumed.
